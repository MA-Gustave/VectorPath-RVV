# VectorPath-RVV — independent AI-assisted development policy

This independent RVV research fork permits AI-assisted implementation, profiling, experiments, test construction and report drafting **with accountable human review, actual verification and rights tracing**. It is not an upstream simdjson submission channel, and the fork is not endorsed by upstream maintainers.

1. The human contributor must understand, inspect and validate generated code and reports; tests and reproducibility records must support claims.
2. Do not copy or reproduce third-party code without its applicable rights and licence obligations. Model-generated text is not proof of human authorship or of exclusive copyright.
3. Document substantial AI assistance and retained design and review evidence for potentially reserved contributions.
4. Keep `PROVENANCE-ADDITIONS.md` synchronized and distinguish earlier permissive licences from new genuinely original qualifying material.
5. **Do not offer or submit qualifying independently rights-reserved original additions for incorporation into official simdjson without the verified rightsholder's separate signed authorization.** No change to earlier Apache/MIT rights is implied.

For the upstream simdjson project's *historical* AI usage policy retained for attribution and comparison, see `UPSTREAM-AI_USAGE_POLICY.md`.
